Description

***ON VIEW SATURDAY 26TH OF SEPTEMBER FROM 11AM TO 1PM FROM OUR BALLYMAKENNY SHOW VILLAGE***

Ballymakenny Park in Drogheda is a prestigious new development brought to you by renowned developers Castlethorn. All homes are beautifully built with stylish interiors and exceptionally spacious and finished to the highest of standards throughout. All homes are energy efficient with a minimum A-rating on the BER scale.

Situated on the North side of Drogheda on Ballymakenny Road this development is the ideal choice for first time buyers, growing families and commuters who want to put down roots in an attractive area. All 3 bedroom homes within this development will qualify for the Help to Buy Scheme & First Home Scheme.

Located on the edge of Drogheda town centre with easy access to a host of amenities nearby to include the M1 Retail Park, Our Lady of Lourdes Hospital, an array of shops, local schools, restaurants, a range of excellent golf clubs and within proximity to some of the best beaches the east coast has to offer - the development is sure to appeal to all types of buyers! The M1 motorway network is only a short drive from Ballymakenny Park via the newly opened Port Access Northern Cross Route (PANCR) and provides fast efficient access for commuters accessing the Motorway to Dublin or Belfast. The Drogheda train station is just a 13-minute drive from Ballymakenny Park and offers a park and ride service.
